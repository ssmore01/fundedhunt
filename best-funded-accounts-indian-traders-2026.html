Best Funded Accounts for Indian Traders in 2026

If you are an Indian trader who has been dreaming about trading with serious capital, 2026 might just be your year. Prop firms have grown massively and the good news is that most of the top ones now fully support Indian traders with easy payment options, USD payouts, and no geographical restrictions.

But here is the problem. There are too many prop firms out there and most review sites just copy paste the same information without actually helping you decide. So we sat down and evaluated each firm honestly based on what actually matters to Indian traders. Rules, payouts, trust, value and support.

Here is our honest breakdown.

🔥 Funding Pips

If there is one firm that consistently impresses Indian traders it is Funding Pips. Founded in Dubai in 2022, they have already paid out over $180 million to traders across the world. What makes them special is their payout flexibility. You can choose to withdraw weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or on demand. No other firm gives you this kind of freedom over your own money.

Their Hot Seat program is one of the most generous scaling offers in the prop trading world right now. Hit Elite Trader status and you unlock a 100% profit split along with up to $2,000,000 in capital. For Indian traders who are serious about building a career in trading, this is a firm worth taking seriously. They offer four challenge models including a 1-Step and Instant Funding option so you can pick what suits your trading style best.

⭐ FundedNext

FundedNext has crossed $158 million in total payouts and has built one of the most active trading communities in the world. What sets them apart for Indian traders is something very unique. They actually pay you 15% of the profits you make during the evaluation phase itself. Most firms make you wait until you are funded. FundedNext rewards you from day one.

Their challenge models include Stellar, Express and Evaluation accounts with capital going up to $200,000 and profit splits reaching up to 95%. Payment support for Indian traders is smooth and their customer service has a good reputation. If you want a firm with a strong track record and a feature that feels genuinely different, FundedNext is worth your attention.

🐐 GOAT Funded Trader

With over 240,000 traders worldwide and a 4.8 star rating on Trustpilot from more than 5,000 reviews, GOAT Funded Trader has built serious credibility in a very short time. Their most talked about feature is the 100% refundable evaluation fee. Pass your challenge and you get your entire challenge fee back with your first payout. For Indian traders who are cautious about spending money on challenges, this removes a big mental barrier.

They also have a Pay Later option where you can start a challenge for just $5 upfront and pay the rest only after you pass. Their payout guarantee is bold too. If your withdrawal is not processed within 2 business days, GOAT pays you an extra $1,000.

🫐 Blueberry Funded

Blueberry Funded is one of the most unique prop firms in the industry because it is backed by Blueberry Markets, an ASIC regulated Australian broker with over 9 years of operation and 50,000 plus traders. Most prop firms are standalone businesses. Blueberry Funded is backed by a real regulated broker which gives it a level of credibility that most others simply do not have.

They offer 7 different challenge types including a Synthetic challenge, a dedicated Stock Challenge with over 1,000 US equity CFDs, and two Instant Funded models. Accounts can scale up to $2,000,000 with profit splits rising to 90%. For Indian traders who care about safety and regulation, Blueberry Funded is a very strong choice.

🏠 The Funded Room

The Funded Room is special for one reason that no other firm can claim. It was founded by Mayank Raj, the creator of The Trade Room YouTube channel which has over 2 million subscribers and is one of the biggest trading education communities in India. The firm launched in February 2026 and has already built a massive following from the Indian trading community.

They offer Instant Funding, One Step and Two Step challenge models on MT5 with 1:100 leverage. The rules are straightforward, there is no time limit on challenges and the profit split is 80%. For Indian traders who follow Mayank Raj or want a firm founded by someone who genuinely understands the Indian trading mindset, The Funded Room feels personal.

💼 Funded Firm

Funded Firm is a solid choice for traders who want clean rules and a firm that does not complicate things. They offer accounts from $5,000 up to $200,000 with competitive profit splits and a straightforward evaluation process. Their rules are trader friendly with no restrictions on trading styles and their payout system is reliable.

For Indian traders who are just getting started with prop firms and want something simple and trustworthy without too much noise, Funded Firm is a great starting point.

Which Firm Should You Choose?

Honestly it depends on where you are in your trading journey. If you are just starting out and want the safest option, go with GOAT Funded Trader for the fee refund or The Funded Room for the Indian community backing. If you are an experienced trader who wants the best scaling and payout flexibility, Funding Pips is hard to beat. If you want the most credible and regulated option, Blueberry Funded stands out clearly. And if you want to earn during your evaluation itself, FundedNext is the one.

The best part is you do not have to figure this out alone. Happy hunting. 🐾
